Source: RedemptionEscrow and README.en.md §5. STALLED / DISPUTED have no administrator override.
07
RETURN
Operator non-response is finalized, or the handoff deadline passes.
Tokens return through finalize / reclaim.
HOLD
Provider non-response → STALLED. Holder dispute → DISPUTED.
Tokens stay locked pending offchain resolution.
BURN
Provider delivery claim is proven; holder acknowledges or dispute window expires.
After expiry in DELIVERED, anyone can call burn.
The dispute window begins when the escrow accepts the proof—not at an older delivery anchor.
